Redesign the GUI. A side rail replaces the header, and the test run, its history and the viewport sit in three columns. Steps keep the console output they caused, a failed test opens with a summary that leads to the failing step, and arrow keys move between steps and switch between before and after. The rail links to the documentation, and the empty state to the guides for getting started and CI snapshots. Geist and JetBrains Mono are bundled, so the GUI still loads nothing from other hosts.
